Keeping it secret

Will she know I hired a photographer?

No. She won’t know I’m there to shoot the proposal until after the proposal starts. I arrive early, position myself well back, and shoot on a long lens looking like any other person at a lookout with a camera.

Where do you actually hide?

It depends on the spot — behind a tree line, on a restaurant deck, on the next headland over, or simply thirty metres back in a crowd. I scout the location beforehand so I know exactly where I’ll be standing and where you should stand.

What if she spots you?

In 200+ proposals it’s never given the game away. Even if someone clocks a person with a camera at a Sydney lookout, that’s the least suspicious thing in the world. The giveaway is usually a nervous partner, not me.

Can family or friends be hidden nearby too?

Absolutely, and it makes for some of the best photos. We’ll plan where they wait and the signal for them to walk in — usually about ninety seconds after the yes.

Planning it

What if I don’t have a location or plan yet?

Most people don’t. Bring the ring and an idea — or no idea at all — and we’ll build it together on the free call. After 200+ proposals across Sydney I know where the light falls, where the crowds don’t, and where I can hide.

How far in advance should I book?

Depending on season, my schedule is often packed, so the earlier the booking, the better. However, most proposals are within two weeks of the initial call.

Can I order things from the full production package à la carte?

Yes, of course. You don’t have to take the whole package to get the pieces you want — plenty of people add just a drone, or just the flowers, on top of standard photography. Tell me which elements you’re after on the call and I’ll price them individually.

Which Sydney locations do you recommend?

Favourites include Sydney Harbour and the Opera House at sunrise, the Bondi to Bronte clifftops, the Royal Botanic Garden, Watsons Bay and South Head, the Northern Beaches headlands, Milk Beach and the Hermitage Foreshore, McKell Park in Darling Point, and Blue Mountains lookouts for couples who’d rather have a valley than an ocean.

Do you travel outside Sydney?

Yes. Blue Mountains, the Central Coast and interstate are all doable with travel factored into the quote. I’ve shot proposals in Porto, Los Angeles, New York, and Geneva, nowhere is off the table.

What time of day works best?

For waterfront photography, the two best times are just after sunrise or just after sunset. Since these times are most popular, these time slots go quickly. For indoor or shaded areas like parks, any time between sunrise and sunset will do. Fill out the form below and we’ll get your time slot reserved.

After the yes

How quickly do I get the photos back?

Your full edited gallery lands within 72 hours. Most people want something to post that night, so I’ll usually send a handful of previews the same day — and expedited delivery is available if you need the lot sooner.

Do we get photos of just the two of us afterwards?

Yes, and I’d encourage it. Once the shock wears off you’re both glowing, the light is usually perfect, and it takes another fifteen minutes. Some of the best frames in my gallery come from those minutes.

Can we use the photos for our engagement announcement?

They’re yours — post them, print them, send them to family, put them on the wedding website. No watermarks and no usage restrictions.

The fine print

What’s your cancellation or bad-weather policy?

Life happens. If the weather turns, we reschedule at no extra cost. I’d rather wait for the right moment than force the wrong one.

What if I’m not happy with my session?

You get a full refund, no questions asked. That’s the 100% Happiness Guarantee — I’d rather make it right than have you settle for photos you don’t love. Nobody has taken me up on it yet.

What happens if they say no?

It’s never happened on one of my shoots. If it did, I’d stop shooting immediately, delete anything you asked me to, and leave. You’d get a full refund.

Do you only shoot proposals?

Proposals are the specialty, but I also shoot couples sessions, engagements, weddings and families — any moment between two people worth keeping.
