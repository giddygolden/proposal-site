Where to shoot portraits in Sydney

The portrait locations
I know by heart.

After 200+ sessions across Sydney, I know exactly where the light falls, where the crowds don’t, and where the background won’t fight your face. A few favourites to get you thinking:

  • Sydney Harbour & the Opera House

    Skyline backdrops at sunrise, before the crowds arrive.

  • Bondi to Bronte clifftops

    Dramatic ledges over that famous blue water.

  • Royal Botanic Garden

    Ferns, sandstone and quiet corners minutes from the CBD.

  • Watsons Bay & South Head

    The lighthouse, the harbour, and golden light both ways.

  • Northern Beaches headlands

    Palm Beach, Barrenjoey and long, empty lookouts.

  • Milk Beach & Hermitage Foreshore

    Small, calm, with the skyline floating across the water.

  • McKell Park, Darling Point

    A manicured garden that drops straight into the harbour.

  • CBD rooftops & laneways

    For personal branding — clean, modern, unmistakably Sydney.

Common questions

Before you ask,
here’s what I’d tell you.

How much does a portrait session cost?

A full personal branding day — hair & makeup, multiple outfits, two locations — goes up to $1,450. A standard portrait session (individual, couple or family) starts at $349. Consultations are free — no commitment, just a conversation.

I hate having my photo taken — is that a problem?

It's the most common thing I hear, and it's never a problem. Direction is simple and conversational, and most people relax within the first few minutes.

What if I don’t know what to wear or where to shoot?

Most people don’t. Bring a couple of outfit options (or none at all), and we’ll figure out the look and the location together on the planning call.

How quickly do I get the photos back?

Average turnaround is well under a week. Expedited delivery is available if you need them for a deadline — a launch, a profile update, a print order.

Do you shoot headshots and personal branding, or just families?

Both, plus couples and solo portraits. I also shoot proposals, engagements and weddings — any moment or person worth capturing well.

What’s your cancellation or bad-weather policy?

Life happens. If the weather turns, we’ll reschedule at no extra cost. I’d rather wait for good light than force a session in the rain.

What if I’m not happy with my session?

You get a full refund, no questions asked. That’s the 100% Happiness Guarantee — I’d rather make it right than have you settle for photos you don’t love.
