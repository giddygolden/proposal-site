Common questions

Before you ask,
here’s what I’d tell you.

How short notice can you take?

It varies depending on the date and what I already have booked — there’s no fixed cut-off I can promise here. The quickest way to find out is to run through the quick quiz below or call me directly; I’ll tell you the same day whether I can make your date work.

Do you travel outside Sydney?

Sydney is my confirmed home base, and I also cover the Blue Mountains, Hunter Valley and Central Coast. Let me know your date and location and I’ll confirm the details.

What happens if it rains, or plans change?

Change of plans? No problem. We can reschedule for no charge up to 48 hours before the shoot.

What’s your deposit or cancellation policy?

Change of plans? No problem. We can reschedule for no charge up to 48 hours before the shoot. Deposit details are confirmed with you directly when we lock in your booking.

How quickly do I get my gallery back?

Fast — usually under a week. Expedited delivery is available if you need to share them sooner.

What if I’m not happy with my gallery?

You get a full refund, no questions asked. That’s the 100% Happiness Guarantee — I’d rather make it right than have you settle for photos you don’t love.
