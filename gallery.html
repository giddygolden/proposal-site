Five proposals, start to finish

Every one of these
was a surprise.

In each set below, the person being proposed to had no idea a photographer was there. I arrive early, stay out of sight, and shoot from a distance until the moment lands — then step in for the celebration.

Christian & Steph — the dock at golden hour

Cottage Point · Hawkesbury River

A floating jetty, forty candles and a basket of white roses, set up while they were still on the boat. I shot the whole thing from the restaurant deck behind a plumeria tree — she had no idea anyone was watching until the champagne came out.

Runa & Elle — a heart built from roses

Balmoral Beach · Sydney Harbour

Two thousand red roses shaped into a heart on the sand, a neon sign inside it, and a violinist playing off to one side. They both cried. So did two strangers walking their dog.

Aaron & Mai — a picnic and a cliff edge

Barrenjoey Headland · Palm Beach

The climb up Barrenjoey is the cover story — a picnic basket at the top, a view down the whole peninsula, and me a hundred metres back with a long lens looking like another hiker taking landscape shots.

Joseph & Lin — the dog was in on it

North Head · Manly

She was holding the dog when he knelt, which is why every frame has a very confused bichon in it. Blankets, cushions and hydrangeas set up on the sandstone, with Manly glowing across the water behind them.

Mahan & Sara — a beach only reachable by boat

Store Beach · Sydney Harbour

Store Beach can only be reached by water, which makes it the easiest secret in Sydney to keep. Rose petals, a chalkboard sign, and her family waiting around the headland to walk in the second she said yes.
